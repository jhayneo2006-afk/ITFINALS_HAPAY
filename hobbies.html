My Hobbies

Things I love to do

🎸 Guitar

I play the guitar to relax and express my creativity. I’ve been practicing for a few years, and I enjoy learning new chords, improving my finger techniques, and playing along with the songs I like.

🏸 Badminton

Badminton is my husband's favorite sport, and over time it became a shared hobby for us. We enjoy playing together on weekends — it keeps us active, competitive, and connected. It's a great way to bond while staying fit.

📖 Reading Books

I love getting lost in a good book. Whether it's fiction, self-help, or inspirational stories, reading helps me expand my perspective and unwind after a long day. It's a quiet hobby that feeds my curiosity and imagination.

🎶 Listening to Old Music

There's something timeless about classic songs — they carry memories and emotions that modern music sometimes can't match. I enjoy listening to old OPM and classic hits that remind me of simpler, meaningful moments in life.

Why Hobbies Matter to Me

Hobbies help me stay motivated and balanced. Whether it's strumming the guitar, rallying on the badminton court with my husband, getting lost in a book, or letting old music take me back in time — each hobby gives me a way to recharge, connect, and appreciate the little joys in life.
